# Business Findings Snapshot

- Paid revenue in the supplied cleaned dataset is **250,380.00**.
- Refund value is **120,983.00**, so net collected revenue is **129,397.00**.
- There are **965 active** and **130 churned** subscriptions; observed subscription churn is **9.57%**.
- The top 10 customers contribute approximately **6.98%** of paid revenue, making customer concentration a useful management monitoring metric.
- The dashboard separates commercial performance (revenue), customer health (churn), product behavior (usage) and service operations (support) so business questions can be investigated across functions.

## Interpretation rule
These are descriptive findings from the supplied dataset. They do not establish causal relationships; product usage, support activity and churn should be investigated with cohort analysis or controlled analysis before claiming causation.
