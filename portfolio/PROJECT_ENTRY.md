# Resume / Portfolio Project Entry

## SaaS Business Performance & Customer Analytics
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, SQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI, Excel

- Analyzed 1,500 customer records, 1,358 subscriptions, 2,813 payment transactions, 22,702 product-usage events, and 716 support tickets to evaluate SaaS business performance.
- Cleaned and validated relational business data using Python/Pandas, including duplicate checks, missing-value checks, date validation, and relationship integrity.
- Used SQL and Python to analyze revenue trends, customer value, subscription churn, plan performance, product engagement, and support operations.
- Developed reproducible KPIs and Power BI reporting for executive performance, customer/subscription health, and product/support analysis.
- Translated analytical results into business observations and investigation areas without overstating causality.
